Privacy Policy

App: Rental Time  |  Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

Rental Time collects only the information necessary to provide its core features:

  • QuickBooks Online financial data (P&L reports) fetched via OAuth 2.0 with your explicit authorization
  • Time entries and property information you log within the app, stored locally on your device
  • Google account information (name, email) used solely for authentication

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Display your rental property income and expense summaries
  • Track time spent managing your rental properties
  • Provide tax preparation support through hour and financial summaries

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

3. QuickBooks Data

Rental Time connects to QuickBooks Online using Intuit's OAuth 2.0 protocol. We access only the accounting scope required to retrieve P&L reports. Access tokens are stored securely on your device using encrypted storage. We do not store your QuickBooks data on any external server.

4. Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device. No personal financial information is transmitted to or stored on third-party servers, except as required to fetch data directly from QuickBooks Online and Bankrate via your own authorized sessions.

5. Data Retention

You may disconnect QuickBooks at any time from within the app, which revokes our access tokens. You may delete all local app data by uninstalling the app.

6. Security

We use industry-standard encryption (iOS Secure Enclave / Keychain) to protect stored tokens and credentials.
